Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Exact Certificates for Unimodality of Forest Independence Polynomials: A consolidated proof manuscript and reproducible supplement”** by **Tong Zhang**.

The manuscript presents a complete proof candidate for unimodality of the independence sequence of every finite simple forest. It combines a structural and rational-certificate argument for all forests on at most 60 vertices with a quantitative hard-core argument for all orders at least 59. The manuscript distinguishes unimodality from log-concavity and explains why the included order-100 relaxation obstruction is not a graph counterexample. It cites the recent sufficiently-large-forest result of Fang, Lu, Nevo, Yao and Zheng and does not substitute its unspecified threshold for the explicit quantitative chain.

The submission includes the LaTeX source, both original certificate archives, the mathematical dependency map, exact reproduction scripts, and fresh audit evidence with its limitations stated. The detailed inherited analytic proofs, some in Chinese, are essential supplements to the English manuscript. Numerical optimization is not part of certificate acceptance. No completed Lean formalization or prior peer-review acceptance is claimed. AI assistance in review and preparation is disclosed in the manuscript.

I would welcome a rigorous assessment of the mathematical reductions, the inherited structural lemmas, and the exact certificate acceptance procedures.
